Return & Exchange Policy

Simple and fair — the same honesty as our pricing.

7 din ke andar — delivery ke din se 7 din tak aap suit exchange karwa sakte hain.

Sharaait (Conditions)

Suit unstiched, unwashed aur bila-istemal hona chahiye — original packing ke sath. Kata ya sila huwa suit wapas nahi hoga, kyunke wo dobara nahi bik sakta.

Defect ya ghalat item

Agar suit me koi defect ho (daagh, phata huwa, rang ka wazeh farq) ya ghalat item bhej diya gaya ho — to foran exchange, bila kisi charges ke. Chahein to cash refund bhi le sakte hain (bank transfer ke zariye).

Pasand ki tabdeeli

Defect na ho, bas design ya rang pasand na aaye — to bhi 7 din me exchange ho jayega (barabar ya zyada qeemat ke suit se; farq ada kar ke). Is surat me delivery charges customer ke zimme honge.

Wholesale orders

Bulk orders me defect wale pieces ki foran tabdeeli hogi. Agar poori lot me koi bara masla ho to baat-cheet se hal nikala jayega — hum dukandaaron ke sath khare hain.

Claim ka tareeqa

WhatsApp 0300 8653797 par apna order number aur suit ki tasveer bhejein. Hum 24 ghante me jawab denge aur exchange ka intezam karenge.

Koi Sawal?

Policy ke bare me kuch poochna ho to WhatsApp par poochein.
